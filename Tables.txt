Tab → Move to next cell

Shift+Tab → Move to previous cell

Alt+Home → First cell in row

Alt+End → Last cell in row

Alt+Page Up/Page Down → First/Last cell in column

Alt+Shift+Enter → Merge cells

Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S → Split cells

Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D → Distribute rows evenly

Ctrl+Alt+Shift+E → Distribute columns evenly
